# ShiftImpact OS™ Growth Intelligence Layer™
# Market Signal Intelligence Knowledge Base v1.0

## Purpose

The Growth Intelligence Layer™ detects meaningful business moments that indicate potential strategic opportunities. The system does not monitor companies simply because they are active. It identifies:

> "When does a company enter a moment where stronger decisions, clearer narratives, better communication or strategic alignment may create business value?"

---

## Core Principle — Activity Detection → Opportunity Intelligence

Traditional monitoring:
```
Company announced something → Record announcement
```

ShiftImpact intelligence:
```
Company announced something
↓ Understand why this moment matters
↓ Identify possible business tension
↓ Identify strategic opportunity
↓ Recommend ShiftImpact intervention
```

---

## The 5-Question Intelligence Model

Every detected signal must answer:

1. **What happened?** — Identify the business event
2. **Why does this matter now?** — Growth phase, reputation moment, market entry, leadership transition
3. **What tension may exist?** — Growth faster than clarity? Recognition not amplified? Expansion creating consistency gaps?
4. **What opportunity exists?** — Brand narrative, strategic clarity, stakeholder comms, marketing alignment
5. **Which ShiftImpact capability applies?** — Signal → tension → module → intervention

---

## AI Decision Rule

Claude must NEVER conclude: *"Company needs marketing."*

Claude must conclude: *"Based on this business moment, there may be a strategic communication, positioning, alignment or growth opportunity worth exploring."*

Claude must NEVER invent company needs, assume budget, or recommend outreach without qualification.

## Signal Taxonomy

### Category 1 — Growth Moments Intelligence

Signals indicating growth, expansion or scaling phase. Creates pressure between ambition, market perception, customer understanding, internal capability.

**1.1 Funding & Investment**
Detect: Seed, Series A/B/C+, VC, PE, strategic investment, government grants, expansion funding
Tension: "Does the market understand the company? Is positioning clear enough? Can communication scale with ambition?"
Opportunities: Founder Growth Diagnostic, positioning clarity, growth narrative, market entry strategy, brand architecture

**1.2 Business Expansion**
Detect: New market entry, regional/international expansion, new customer segments, verticals, revenue streams, business units
Tension: "Existing story may not fit future ambition. Customer understanding may lag expansion."
Opportunities: Market Expansion Readiness Audit, strategic narrative, customer communication framework

**1.3 Physical Expansion**
Detect: New stores/branches/offices/factories, experience centres, production facilities
Tension: "Operational growth requires experience and communication consistency."
Opportunities: Customer experience strategy, growth communication, brand consistency framework

**1.4 Partnership**
Detect: Strategic partnerships, JVs, technology partnerships, distribution agreements, ecosystem collaborations
Tension: "Partnership value exists but may require stronger storytelling."
Opportunities: Partnership narrative, stakeholder communication, launch platform

---

### Category 2 — Recognition Moments Intelligence

External validation signals. Transform achievement into reputation value.

**2.1 Awards**
Detect: Brand, marketing, advertising, industry, innovation, business, excellence awards
Tension: "Company has credibility but may not have converted recognition into influence."
Opportunities: Brand storytelling, reputation amplification, thought leadership

**2.2 ESG & Sustainability Recognition**
Detect: ESG awards, sustainability awards, green certifications, social impact recognition, responsible business awards
Tension: "Company has ESG achievements but may struggle communicating impact clearly."
Opportunities: ESG Storytelling Diagnostic, stakeholder communication, sustainability narrative

**2.3 Employer & Culture Recognition**
Detect: Best employer awards, workplace rankings, culture recognition, talent awards
Opportunities: Employer brand strategy, internal engagement, talent communication

**2.4 Leadership Recognition**
Detect: CEO awards, founder recognition, industry rankings, executive invitations
Opportunities: Executive positioning, leadership narrative, thought leadership

---

### Category 3 — Corporate Milestone Intelligence

Meaningful company achievements or anniversaries.

**3.1 Heritage & Anniversary**
Detect: 5/10/25/50-year anniversaries, heritage celebrations
Tension: "Company has history but may need a stronger future-facing story."

**3.2 Customer Milestones**
Detect: 1M customers, community milestones, user milestones, loyalty achievements

**3.3 Business Achievement**
Detect: Revenue milestones, production milestones, market leadership, operational achievements

---

### Category 4 — Activation Moments Intelligence

Signals of active communication, launching or audience engagement.

**4.1 Launch**
Detect: Product/service launches, brand refresh, rebranding, packaging redesign, campaign launches
Tension: "Company is moving externally but may lack strategic communication alignment."
Opportunities: Launch readiness audit, campaign architecture, message clarity

**4.2 Corporate Events**
Detect: Annual dinners, customer events, partner events, dealer conventions, town halls, leadership conferences

**4.3 Industry Events**
Detect: Conferences, exhibitions, forums, trade shows, speaking opportunities

**4.4 Sponsorship & Activation**
Detect: Sponsorship announcements, creator/influencer partnerships, media collaborations

---

### Category 5 — Leadership Moment Intelligence

Organisational change or leadership transition signals.

**5.1 Executive Appointments**
Detect: CEO, CMO, Marketing Director, country manager, regional leader appointments
Tension: "New leadership may require a refreshed direction or narrative."

**5.2 Founder Transitions**
Detect: Founder stepping back/returning, visibility increase, succession

---

### Category 6 — Competitive & Market Pressure Intelligence

External pressure or changing market conditions.

Detect: Competitor launches, new entrants, category disruption, pricing pressure, consumer behaviour changes, regulation changes, industry disruption
Tension: "Market has changed faster than company response."
Opportunities: Strategic repositioning, market response planning, Decision Page, competitive clarity

---

### Category 7 — Talent & Capability Intelligence

Capability building or organisational maturity signals.

Detect: Hiring campaigns, new marketing/growth/digital/ESG/brand/communications roles
Tension: "Organisation is investing in capability but may need strategic clarity."
Opportunities: Marketing operating model, team alignment, strategic framework
