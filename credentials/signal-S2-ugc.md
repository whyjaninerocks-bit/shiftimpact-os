# Signal 2 — UGC (User Generated Content): Malaysia Market Credential

**Signal definition:** Volume and growth rate of organic, unpaid content created by real consumers about a brand or product — measured as a count of unique pieces (videos, posts, reviews) per campaign period.  
**Gate threshold used in OS:** UGC growing month-on-month after campaign Week 4; minimum 65 pieces tracked at 30-day mark (Cooks demo calibration).  
**Classification:** Lag signal for Awareness stage; lead signal for Conversion stage.

---

## Why UGC is a meaningful signal in Malaysia

### Trust asymmetry is extreme

90% of consumers trust UGC while only 4% trust branded messages. This gap is more pronounced in Southeast Asia where peer recommendation carries cultural weight — particularly within Malay community networks where community validation ("orang dah try") is a purchase trigger, and within Chinese-speaking segments where Xiaohongshu-style community commerce review culture has migrated into TikTok and WhatsApp groups.

Source: [archive.com UGC Statistics 2026](https://archive.com/blog/user-generated-content) · [Webtonic TikTok UGC 2026](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/tiktok-ugc-12-ways-to-leverage-user-generated-content-for-brand-growth-in-2026)

### Malaysian academic evidence: food UGC directly drives purchase intent

A USIM (Universiti Sains Islam Malaysia) study specifically examining UGC on food content through TikTok in the Malaysian market found that audio/video UGC components and food review information directly increase purchase intention. Crucially, informants reported being "more influenced by the virality of the food that encourages them to purchase it whether online or offline." This documents the UGC-to-virality-to-purchase chain in the exact market ShiftImpact OS operates in.

Source: [Mohd Azman & Mustaffa, USIM 2023 — Al-i'lam Journal](https://jcicom.usim.edu.my/index.php/journal/article/view/89)

### UGC performance vs branded content in Malaysia

TikTok UGC generates 10x higher engagement than traditional commercials. Instagram posts with UGC garner 70% more engagement than brand-only content. TikTok leads organic engagement at 2.63% per post — the highest of any major platform. In the Malaysian context where 21.9% of users cite TikTok as their favourite platform specifically for authentic content, UGC is the format least likely to be scrolled past.

Source: [Webtonic TikTok UGC 2026](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/tiktok-ugc-12-ways-to-leverage-user-generated-content-for-brand-growth-in-2026) · [IQFluence Benchmarks 2026](https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/social-media-benchmarks)

### The Xiaohongshu effect on the MY-Chinese segment

Xiaohongshu has carved out dominant reach among urban Chinese-speaking Malaysian female users aged 18–30, specifically as a community commerce and product review platform. This is the same audience that uses TikTok for discovery and WhatsApp groups for validation. UGC authenticity — especially for food and lifestyle products — is this segment's primary trust filter. Brands without UGC presence are effectively invisible to this cohort regardless of ad spend.

Source: [UnRavel Digital Malaysia Social 2025](https://unravel.com.my/our-thoughts/malaysia-social-media-landscape-2025-beyond/)

### 71% of TikTok users discover new products on the platform

Of those, 33% make purchases directly after UGC exposure. In the food category, emotional attachment and perceived relevance created by UGC strengthens purchase intention specifically. This is the mechanism the Cooks campaign activates: recipe-led UGC creates "I can make this" emotional relevance, which is the pre-purchase state for a cooking ingredient brand.

Source: [Webtonic TikTok UGC 2026](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/tiktok-ugc-12-ways-to-leverage-user-generated-content-for-brand-growth-in-2026) · [USIM Study 2023](https://jcicom.usim.edu.my/index.php/journal/article/view/89)

## UGC taxonomy used in ShiftImpact OS

Three tiers tracked; only Tier 1 counts toward the UGC signal gate:

| Tier | Type | Counts toward signal? |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Organic UGC — unprompted, no compensation | Yes |
| Tier 2 | Seeded UGC — gifted product, no creative direction | Partial (0.5 weight) |
| Tier 3 | Paid Creator / KOL — contracted output | No — tracked separately |

Paid KOLs are not UGC. Conflating them inflates the signal and breaks prediction accuracy.

## Signal in the OS

- **Lag signal** for Awareness stage — measures whether the brand has achieved sufficient authentic salience to generate organic recreation
- **Lead signal** for Conversion stage — organic UGC growing MoM is Gate 3 (Retention) threshold
- **Feeds:** Creative Battery assessment, KOL brief quality score, Brand Posture trajectory
