# Signal 3 — Brand Search: Malaysia Market Credential

**Signal definition:** Volume change in branded search queries (Google + TikTok search) for the brand name and product category, measured as % lift vs pre-campaign baseline.  
**Gate threshold used in OS:** 18% brand search lift at campaign midpoint (Week 5–6).  
**Classification:** Lag signal for Awareness stage — confirms social exposure has translated into active inquiry.

---

## Why brand search is the right confirmation signal in Malaysia

### The multi-platform search reality

Traditional Google-only brand search measurement misses the actual Malaysian search landscape. In 2026, Malaysians follow a multi-platform discovery path: TikTok for initial product discovery, Google for detailed research and comparison, Shopee/Lazada for price validation, Instagram for brand authenticity check. Brand search across this stack — not just Google — is the correct measurement frame.

72% of TikTok users search for more information after viewing content — effectively using TikTok as a search engine. For a cooking ingredient brand running recipe-led content, a consumer who watches the video and then searches "Cooks paste recipe" or "Cooks sambal paste" on Google or TikTok is mid-funnel, not top-funnel.

Source: [Newnormz — Search Behaviour Malaysia 2026](https://www.newnormz.com.my/search-behaviour-digital-marketing/) · [TikTok for Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-GB)

### 62.9% of Malaysians research before buying

And search is how they do it. 55.6% use search engines as the primary source of brand information when researching a purchase. 51.3% have visited a brand's website in the previous 30 days. Brand search is not vanity — it is the documented bridge between social exposure and the purchase decision.

Source: [Meltwater Malaysia Digital 2024](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/social-media-statistics-malaysia)

### Brand search = social campaign confirmation

Brand search lift is used in the OS as the verification that paid and earned social activity has generated real-world curiosity, not just scroll-past impressions. When TikTok content runs and brand search volume does not lift within 2 weeks, it indicates one of: wrong audience targeting, wrong content format, or brand name insufficient recall. This makes it a diagnostic signal, not just a vanity metric.

83% of Malaysian shoppers switched brands in 2026. Brand search lift measures whether a campaign created enough salience to enter the consideration set of brand-switchers.

Source: [Accio Consumer Trends Malaysia 2026](https://www.accio.com/business/consumer-trends-in-malaysia)

### Gen Z search migration: social platforms over Google

A projected 60% of Gen Z Malaysians will start their discovery on TikTok/Instagram rather than Google. For food and FMCG brands targeting the 18–34 segment, brand search measurement must include TikTok search volume, not just Google Search Console data. The OS captures both where available.

Source: [UnRavel Digital Malaysia Social 2025](https://unravel.com.my/our-thoughts/malaysia-social-media-landscape-2025-beyond/)

### Attribution in a cookieless environment

As third-party cookies erode and Meta's conversion API becomes the dominant measurement approach, brand search volume stands as one of the few durable, platform-independent signals that can be read without vendor data access. A client who cannot provide Meta CAPI data can still provide Google Search Console — brand search is the fallback that keeps the OS working without full data access.

---

## What brand search does NOT measure

Brand search is a lag signal — it confirms that prior signals (save rate, UGC growth) have created sufficient salience, but it does not predict conversion. A brand search spike that is not followed by retail velocity lift (or GrabMart add-to-cart data) indicates awareness without purchase follow-through, which points to a price, availability, or in-store placement problem, not a content problem.

---

## Signal in the OS

- **Lag signal** — fires at Week 5–6, confirming Awareness stage impact
- **Gate role:** 18% lift is the threshold before the OS recommends investing in conversion-stage content (influencing the brief for Week 6+)
- **Feeds:** Brand Posture trajectory, campaign health overall score
- **Diagnostic fork:** Brand search lifts but retail doesn't → distribution or pricing issue flagged
